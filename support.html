How can we help?

Our support team is available 24/7 to assist you with any queries regarding matches, wallets, or KYC.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I withdraw my winnings? expand_more

You need to complete your KYC verification first. Once verified, go to the Wallet section, click on Withdraw, enter the amount (Min ₹50) and your bank details.

My KYC is pending for a long time? expand_more

KYC verification usually takes 24-48 hours. If it has been longer than that, please contact our support team via WhatsApp with your registered email.

How are fantasy points calculated? expand_more

Points are calculated based on the real-life performance of your selected players. Captains get 2x points and Vice-Captains get 1.5x points.
